I am a physical oceanographer specialized in vertical turbulent mixing and machine learning. In my work, I have explored how various turbulence closures impact the representation of local mixing processes in global ocean models, I calibrated general circulation model parameters using Bayesian optimization, and I am currently expanding the vertical mixing scheme of the Modular Ocean Model with a neural network component to enhance its physical realism.

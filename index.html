About Me

I am an engaged and creative climate researcher with an international perspective, currently working as a Postdoctoral Researcher at Princeton University. My research focuses on upper ocean turbulence, climate modeling, and the development of machine learning approaches for coupled Earth system modeling.

Throughout my academic career, I have worked extensively with climate modeling and cross-disciplinary oceanography. I am passionate about improving our understanding of ocean processes and their representation in climate models, with a particular focus on high-resolution storm-resolving models and automated tuning techniques.
